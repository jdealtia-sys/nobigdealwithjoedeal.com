## What to SKIP (from the agency pitch — their own slides prove it)

- **Geo meta tags** (`geo.region`, `geo.position`, `ICBM`) — Google has ignored these
  for 15+ years. The "example" they showed you was another client's Austin TX site
  with dozens of stacked geo tags. Your site already has the real thing: RoofingContractor
  schema with coordinates, areaServed, and your GAF credential — now validating with
  **0 errors / 0 warnings** (fixed 2026-07-06, PR #879).
- **Bought backlinks** — your current backlink profile in their own report is
  toplikevideo.com, metamagic.top, and a fake Fiverr testimonial page. That junk IS
  what cheap link building produces. All nofollow, all worthless.
- **"Toxic link removal"** — Google ignores nofollow spam. Disavow only if Search
  Console ever shows a manual action. Don't pay anyone to "clean" 11 links.
- **"Raising your Authority Score"** — a Semrush marketing metric, not a Google
  ranking factor. It rises on its own as the real citations above accumulate.

## Reviews playbook (the highest-leverage local ranking factor, full stop)

Your plumbing is already live — verified today:
- `nobigdealwithjoedeal.com/r` → 302s straight to your Google review form ✓
- `/yardsign` → 302s to your GBP profile ✓
- 7 print QR codes already made (yard signs, door hangers, inspect cards)

The cadence:
1. **Ask in person at job completion** — "if I earned it, a Google review is the
   biggest thank-you there is" — then **text the /r link the same day** while the
   job's fresh. (Your CRM's texting panel can send it with one tap.)
2. **Respond to every review within 48 hours** — including any bad one (calm +
   specific beats defensive; responses are ranking signal AND sales copy).
3. **One GBP post per week** — your April storm-check post is exactly the right
   pattern. Rotate: storm/seasonal tip → finished-job photo → offer/reminder.
4. **3–5 job photos per month uploaded to GBP** (real jobs, real addresses' towns
   in the caption: "Roof replacement in Mason, OH").
5. Target: 2–4 new reviews/month, steady. Steady beats bursts.
