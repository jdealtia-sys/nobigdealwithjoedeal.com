# Lexington / Fayette County contractor setup — research brief (2026-08-26)

Jo's queue item from the Central Kentucky expansion: *"confirm any
Lexington–Fayette / local permit or licensing requirements before the first
Lexington job."* This is the desk-research half, web-verified 2026-08-26 —
sources linked, one question deliberately left open for a phone call. Nothing
here is legal advice; the two phone numbers below settle anything ambiguous
in minutes.

## The one-paragraph answer

Kentucky has **no state-level roofing license** — roofing is not one of the
DHBC-licensed trades (electrical/plumbing/HVAC are). What Lexington *does*
require is local: **LFUCG contractor registration** (an ordinance —
unregistered contractors cannot pull permits) which itself needs an **LFUCG
occupational business license** and a **certificate of insurance**. Both are
routine paperwork, not exams. One open question — whether a like-for-like
residential reroof needs a building permit at all — has conflicting public
answers and needs one call to Building Inspection.

## Verified: state level

- **No roofing license required in Kentucky.** Roofing operates without a
  state contractor license; DHBC licenses electrical, plumbing and HVAC, not
  roofing. ([ServiceTitan KY guide](https://www.servicetitan.com/licensing/roofing/kentucky),
  [contractorrequirements.com/roofer/kentucky](https://contractorrequirements.com/roofer/kentucky/))
- **⚠ Watch item — KY HB 150 (introduced 2026-01-07)** proposes a state
  roofing-contractor licensing system under DHBC. In committee as of this
  research, **not law**. If it passes, this runbook's state section is stale —
  re-check before relying on it in 2027.
- Voluntary: KRCA certified-contractor membership exists (~1 yr experience,
  insurance proof, $10k bond, CE hours) — marketing value only, not required.

## Verified: LFUCG (Lexington-Fayette) requirements

Registration is required by ordinance to pull permits. Steps, from
[lexingtonky.gov — How to register as a contractor](https://www.lexingtonky.gov/working/bids-quotes-rfps/how-register-contractor):

1. **Occupational business license** — Revenue office, 218 E. Main St.,
   2nd floor, Lexington, KY 40507 · **(859) 258-3340**. (NBD's office is
   outside Fayette County, so the $10 Home Office Certificate step marked
   "not required if location is outside Fayette County" should not apply —
   confirm when calling.)
2. **Certificate of Insurance** — general liability; list
   `LFUCG, 200 E. Main St., Lexington, KY 40507` as certificate holder.
   **Solo-operator note:** the application has a "Workers Comp Exempt"
   question — a company with no employees selects **YES**. That's the
   it's-just-me-Joe path; have the GL certificate reissued with LFUCG as
   holder before applying.
3. **Contractor Registration Application** — online at
   [lexingtonky.gov/permits](https://www.lexingtonky.gov/permits) →
   Building Inspection → Contractor Registration Application; upload the
   occupational license + COI.
- Building Inspection: 101 E. Vine St., Lexington, KY 40507 ·
  **(859) 258-3770** · buildinginspection@lexingtonky.gov · Mon–Fri 8–5.
- Renewal cadence: **not stated on the page** — ask when calling.

## OPEN — settle by phone before the first tear-off

**Does a like-for-like residential reroof need a building permit in
Fayette County?** Public sources conflict: a cost-tracking site claims
Fayette requires a roofing permit (~$141) for replacements, while a
Lexington-area roofer's own blog says
[no permit is needed to reshingle a residential roof](https://jacobsladderky.com/2022/07/27/do-you-need-a-permit-to-replace-or-reshingle-a-roof/)
in Lexington/Nicholasville. Structural work (sheathing/deck repair) is more
likely to trigger one either way. **One call to (859) 258-3770 answers it**
— ask for the residential-reroof rule + whether registration alone suffices
when no permit is triggered, and note the answer here with a date.
