What You Get With a Full Roof Replacement

A full roof replacement isn't just about improving appearance; it's a long-term solution that protects your home from water damage, mold, and structural decay. Our roof replacement services start with a free roof inspection to evaluate your current condition. If needed, we'll handle structural repairs before installing brand-new materials for long-term durability. We specialize in shingle roof replacement, using trusted brands like Owens Corning and Atlas.

If you prefer metal roof replacement for added lifespan and wind resistance, we can do that too. We also offer roof replacement options that boost curb appeal and provide solid weather resistance. Our new roof installation process includes removing all old roofing materials, inspecting for any hidden damage, and preparing the deck for a secure install. Every residential roof replacement is customized to fit the structure, weather conditions, and budget. We take pride in offering reliable roof inspection services to make sure nothing gets missed.

We also assist with insurance claims, helping homeowners maximize their coverage. If your roof damage qualifies, we'll provide documentation and support to make the process smoother. Whether you're replacing an aged roof or dealing with storm damage, our team delivers results you can count on. From flashing to ridge caps, every detail matters. Trust us to provide energy-efficient and weather-tight solutions built to last.

Why Homeowners Trust Our Process

When it comes to protecting your home, a properly installed roof makes all the difference. At Oaks Roofing & Construction in Goshen, OH, our focus is on delivering a detailed, personalized process that ensures lasting results. We begin with a free inspection to assess the roof's current condition. From there, we walk you through options for materials, color, and performance. Whether it's traditional shingles or impact-resistant alternatives, we install what best suits your home and budget.

During the tear-off phase, we remove all damaged or worn materials to expose the decking. Our team inspects for soft spots, water intrusion, or compromised framing, and we make structural repairs as needed. Once the deck is sound, we install underlayment, flashing, and your new roofing system. We work efficiently, minimize disruption, and keep your property clean during the project. In addition to retail projects, we also help with storm damage repairs and insurance-related roof replacement services.

Our team handles documentation and supports you through every step of the claims process. Our clients appreciate that we take repairs seriously too. We won't upsell a replacement if a repair is all that's needed. That mindset is what sets us apart.
